Overview

SEO Insights ("we," "us," or "our") publishes SEO blogs and provides free tools such as the Bulk URL Opener, Diff Checker, and Online Text Editor, alongside SEO services for websites and businesses. This policy applies to everyone who visits theseoinsights.com or uses any tool, form, or service on the site.

By using this website, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the site.

Information we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Contact details, such as your name and email address, when you reach out via support@theseoinsights.com or a contact form.
  • Any project details or website URLs you share with us when requesting an SEO service.
  • Text or URLs you paste into a free tool, such as the Diff Checker or Online Text Editor, in order to generate a result.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited, collected through server logs and analytics.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, used only to understand general audience trends.
  • Referring website and search terms that led you to our pages, where available.

How our free tools handle data

Our free tools are built to do their job without collecting more than necessary.

Bulk URL Opener only reads the list of links you paste in, in your own browser, to open them in new tabs. Diff Checker and Online Text Editor process the text you enter locally in your browser session to show you a result. We do not permanently store the URLs or text you submit to these tools, and we do not sell tool input to third parties.

If a future tool update requires temporary server-side processing, we will only retain what is needed to return your result, and we will update this policy accordingly.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website, blog, and tools.
  • To respond to enquiries and deliver the SEO services you request.
  • To understand how visitors use our content and tools, so we can prioritise fixes and new features.
  • To protect the site against abuse, spam, and security threats.
  • To send service-related updates, only where you have asked to hear from us.

Cookies & tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember basic preferences, keep the site running smoothly, and understand aggregate traffic patterns through analytics.

  • Essential cookies that let core site features, like the mobile navigation menu, function correctly.
  • Analytics cookies that help us see which pages, blog posts, and tools are most useful.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Doing so may affect how some parts of the site behave.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Service providers who help us run analytics, hosting, or email delivery, under agreements that limit how they can use your data.
  • Authorities, where required to comply with a legal obligation or to protect the rights and safety of our users.
  • A successor organisation, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you where required by law.

Data security & retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and request a copy.
  • Request that we correct or delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications at any time.
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, certain uses of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@theseoinsights.com and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Children's privacy

Our website and tools are intended for a general business and creator audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Third-party links

Our blog and tools pages may link to external websites we do not control. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party sites, and we encourage you to review their policies before sharing information with them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or for legal reasons. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page whenever changes are made, so please check back periodically.
